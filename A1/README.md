# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St and Pedestrain Counter (hourly counts, 2019)
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/.../2z5v-ecg8  (pulled YYYY-MM-DD)
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain (as listed on the Seattle Open Data portal). Free to use, share and modify; the City asks for attribution and provides the data "as is."

## Questions I asked
- Q1: When is the trail busiest and quietest?
- Q2: Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day?
- Q3: Is there evidence of commuting, comparing weekdays vs. weekends?
- Q4: Do mornings and evenings flow in opposite directions?
